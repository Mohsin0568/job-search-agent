SEARCH AND FILTERING RULES — follow all of these strictly:

1. RECENCY: Only include job postings that were posted within the last 7 days from today's date.
   Discard any posting older than 7 days. If the posting date cannot be determined, exclude it.

2. DESIGNATION MATCHING: From the resume, identify the candidate's most recent job title/designation.
   Only return postings for:
   - The same designation or an equivalent title at the target company, OR
   - One level up (i.e. a more senior version of that role, e.g. Senior, Lead, Principal, Head of).
   Ignore roles that are junior to the candidate's current level or unrelated to their career track.

3. SKILLS MATCHING: Extract the key technical and domain skills listed in the resume.
   Only include a job posting if it requires or strongly matches at least the majority of those skills.
   Discard postings where the required skills significantly differ from the candidate's profile.

4. ATS SCORE: For each job posting that passes the above filters, calculate an ATS (Applicant Tracking
   System) score as an integer from 0 to 100 representing how well the candidate's resume matches the
   job description. Use the following breakdown when calculating the score:
   - Skills match (40 points): how many of the required skills in the job description are present in the resume.
   - Experience & seniority match (30 points): how closely the candidate's years of experience and
     seniority level align with what the role requires.
   - Designation / title match (20 points): how closely the candidate's current or recent title matches
     the job title.
   - Education & certifications match (10 points): how well the candidate's qualifications meet the
     job's requirements.
   Sum the points and return the total as atsScore. A score of 80 or above indicates a strong match.
